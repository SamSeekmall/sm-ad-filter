## Detailed description

SM Clean View blocks ads and trackers on the websites you visit, and gives you simple controls for the times a page needs a hand.

PROTECTION THAT STAYS OUT OF THE WAY
• Blocks ads, trackers and known malware sites with the uBlock Origin Lite engine and its built-in filter lists (EasyList, EasyPrivacy, uBlock filters and more).
• Filtering is done by the browser itself, so the extension uses very little memory or CPU while you browse.
• Optional Chinese-site rules for ads common on mainland-China websites.

ONE-TAP CONTROLS IN THE TOOLBAR
• See at a glance whether the current site is protected.
• Choose how strict each site is: Off, Light, Standard or Strong. One tap applies it.
• Pause a site for 10 minutes, 1 hour or until the browser closes. It resumes by itself.
• Hide an element just once, or always, with a point-and-click picker.

PAGE LOOKS WRONG? FIX IT STEP BY STEP
• Pause the site from the popup, look at the page, and say whether it works now.
• If filtering caused it, lower that one site by one level; other sites are not affected.
• If it didn't, resume protection, or create a feedback report with one click.

EVERYTHING CAN BE UNDONE
• The last 10 setting changes are kept on your device and can be undone.
• Export and import your settings as a file. Imports are previewed first and roll back automatically if anything fails.
• Optional sync through your browser account, off by default.

MORE
• Reduce annoyances: filter overlays, notification prompts and social widgets.
• Optional automatic rejection of OneTrust and Cookiebot cookie banners. It only ever clicks "Reject all".
• English and Chinese interface.

PRIVACY
SM Clean View collects nothing. There are no analytics, no telemetry and no SeekMall servers. Your settings stay in your browser. Full policy: https://samseekmall.github.io/sm-ad-filter/privacy.html

GOOD TO KNOW
• Browser pages and extension stores can't be filtered by any extension.
• Some video sites deliver ads inside the video stream; those can't always be removed.
• Please don't run several ad-blocking extensions at once.

SM Clean View is an independent modified version of uBlock Origin Lite by Raymond Hill and contributors, released under GPL-3.0. It is not an official uBlock Origin product. Source code: https://github.com/SamSeekmall/sm-ad-filter
